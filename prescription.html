How It Works

1

Upload Prescription

Take a clear photo of your prescription or upload a scanned copy. Ensure all text is readable and the image is well-lit.

2

Our AI uses advanced OCR (Optical Character Recognition) to extract text and understand medical terminology from your prescription.

3

Extracted Information

The AI identifies medications, dosages, frequencies, and doctor information, then organizes them in an easy-to-read format.

4

Save & Manage

Save extracted medications to your profile, set reminders, and check for drug interactions with your current medications.
